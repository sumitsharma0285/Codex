1) Current Profile Audit

What Is Working

  • Authentic warm visual identity (home/studio, natural light, real tone).
  • Strong Reel instinct; most content is short-form video.
  • Good highlights structure: Testimonials, Classes, Corporate, Postpartum, Kids.
  • Wide content breadth: yoga, Ayurveda, mindful eating, postpartum, corporate wellness.

Critical Gaps

  • Follower-to-following ratio (636 vs 705) weakens perceived authority.
  • No link in bio (missed leads: calls, classes, testimonials).
  • Bio message is overloaded with multiple offers and unclear CTA.
  • Reach plateau: most reels in ~600–1,500 views; discovery not scaling beyond current circle.

Quick Structural Fixes

  • Keep username and profile photo (already strong).
  • Clean up non-engaging follows to improve profile authority perception.
  • Pin 3 posts: intro reel, best performer, testimonial reel.
  • Create CTA hierarchy: DM keyword + one primary booking action.

3) Content Strategy (4-Pillar Mix)

Pillar 1 — Quick Holistic Tips 40%

Ayurveda + Yoga “surprise hooks” based on her top performers.

  • “You may be drinking water wrong…”
  • “Ancient habit that fixes morning fatigue”

Pillar 2 — Relatable Lifestyle 25%

Mom life + yoga life storytelling for shareability and connection.

  • “My fellow 90s kids…” series
  • “How moms actually fit yoga in real life”

Pillar 3 — Educational Deep Dives 20%

Authority-building explainers, preferably as save-worthy carousels.

  • “5 Morning Ayurvedic rituals” carousel
  • “Postpartum yoga myths debunked”

Pillar 4 — Social Proof / BTS 15%

Client outcomes, class snippets, and personal founder story.

  • Video testimonials
  • “What online class with me looks like” reel

4) Reach & Visibility Engine

Posting Cadence

  • 5 posts/week minimum: 4 Reels + 1 Carousel
  • Best times (IST): 7–9 AM and 8–10 PM
  • Daily stories with polls, questions, behind-the-scenes.

Hook + Hashtag Optimization

  • Every reel needs first-frame on-screen hook (1–3 seconds).
  • Layered hashtags: mega + mid + niche + community tags.
  • Use trending audio within 24–48h where relevant.

Engagement System

  • 15–20 mins pre-post commenting on 10–15 niche accounts.
  • Reply to all comments within first hour after posting.
  • Weekly collab outreach to 5–10 adjacent creators (5K–50K).

5) Client Acquisition Funnel

DM Funnel

  • Use “DM START” as primary call-to-action across content.
  • Create reel: free 20-min discovery call + what it covers.
  • Weekly story sequence: transformation, slot alert, FAQ box.

Lead Magnets + Workshops

  • Offer free PDF: “7-Day Morning Ritual for Busy Moms.”
  • Run ₹99–299 workshop every 4–6 weeks.
  • Use workshop signups to nurture into paid cohorts.

6) Brand Collaboration Readiness

At current size, Kaveri fits the micro-influencer tier—valuable for trusted niche engagement. Prioritize wellness, Ayurveda, yoga props, and kids wellness brands. Start with gifted collaborations, then move to paid deliverables.

  • Build 1-page media kit: bio, audience insights, engagement rate, content examples.
  • Target initial brand categories: Kapiva-style wellness, yoga props, health foods, kids nutrition.
  • Outreach script: short value-led DM/email with clear niche positioning and content offer.

7) 90-Day Roadmap

Month 1 — Foundation

Bio/link optimization, 20-reel batch, 5x weekly cadence, account cleanup, daily engagement routine.

Month 2 — Amplify

Launch one workshop, pitch 5 brands, begin collab posts/lives, start lead-nurture sequence via stories + broadcast.

Month 3 — Convert

Launch paid 4-week cohort, convert workshop leads, collect video testimonials, double down on top-performing formats.

  1. Rewrite bio + add link-in-bio hub.

  3. Post one hook-first reel: “Nobody tells new moms this about postpartum yoga.”
  4. Reply to every comment on last 5 posts.
  5. Post stories daily, even casual behind-the-scenes updates.
